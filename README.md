# ourweddinginvite.github.io

- `/`: Combined Nikah and Reception invitation.
- `/nikah/`: Burgundy-and-cream Nikah invitation, with bride-first names and
  excerpts from Qur'an 4:1 and 2:187. Its type adjustments are in `nikah/style.css`.
- `/reception/`: Independent emerald-and-gold Reception invitation with an
  illuminated arch opening. Its theme is in `reception/style.css`.

All versions retain automatic scrolling, venue links, music and Replay.
The separate Nikah and Reception pages are maintained independently; do not
regenerate them from the combined page or their custom wording and themes will
be lost. The prior Nikah version is saved at the local Git tag
`nikah-before-bride-first-20261003`.
